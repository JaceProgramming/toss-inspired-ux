# Sources and attribution

This guide is an independent reading of material that Toss (Viva Republica) has published. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by Toss.

## How the material was used

- The **ideas** come from the sources below: the principles, the reasoning behind them, and the outcomes Toss reported from its own experiments.
- The **wording, structure, examples and code** in this repository were written for this guide. Text from the sources is not reproduced. Where a Toss case is described, it is summarised with a link to the original.
- **Principle names** ("One Thing", "Value First", "Predictable hint" and so on) are Toss's own and are used to credit them.
- **Numeric defaults** such as motion durations, easing curves, spacing, type sizes and colours are common platform conventions chosen for this guide. They are not Toss's specifications, and the colour palette is not Toss's.
- **No Toss assets** are included: no logos, fonts, icons, emoji, illustrations or design-system components. The TDS documentation was read only to understand how common components are structured.

If you are at Toss and would like something here corrected or removed, please open an issue.
